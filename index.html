01 — ABOUT

Research with
Real-World Impact.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, network security, and efficient machine learning.

I earned my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Concordia University, Montréal. My doctoral research explored generative adversarial networks for robust and scalable intrusion detection systems, addressing challenges such as rare cyberattacks, imbalanced datasets, and computational constraints.

I am particularly interested in translating rigorous AI research into security solutions that are reliable, reproducible, and practical to deploy.
